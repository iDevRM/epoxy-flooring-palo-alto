What Happens When You Call?

Calling about an epoxy floor in Palo Alto takes a few minutes, and there's no pressure at any step.

  1. A few quick questions

    We ask about the space, its size and whether there's old paint, cracking or dampness, so we know what to bring.

  2. We pick a time to visit

    Estimates are booked on weekdays, with Saturday visits by appointment. Most take 20 to 30 minutes.

  3. We inspect, measure and test

    The slab is measured, cracks are noted and moisture is tested. Ask anything you like while we're there.

  4. You get a written quote

    The quote lists the prep, repairs and coating system. Take your time deciding — nobody pushes you to book on the spot.
