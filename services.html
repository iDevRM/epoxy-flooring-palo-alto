Whether it's a 1920s detached garage near downtown, an Eichler in Greenmeadow or a lab bay in the Research Park, the coating is only as good as the prep under it. Here's what each system does and who it's for.

Garage Floor Epoxy

Garage floor epoxy is a flake coating system installed over a diamond-ground slab and sealed with a clear topcoat. It solves the most common garage complaints: dust, oil stains and paint that peels under hot tires.

It's the right call for anyone who parks inside, keeps tools or a second fridge in the garage, or is adding an EV charger. Left alone, stains set deeper into bare concrete and old paint keeps lifting until the whole floor looks worn out.

Read more about garage floor epoxy. Call us today for garage floor epoxy in Palo Alto.

Metallic Epoxy Flooring

Metallic epoxy flooring is a decorative system where metallic pigment is worked by hand in wet resin, creating a marbled, three-dimensional look. It turns plain gray concrete into a floor that looks finished and intentional.

Homeowners pick it for garage gyms, studios and home offices, and businesses use it in showrooms and lobbies. Without it, a converted space with a bare slab still looks and feels like storage.

Read more about metallic epoxy flooring. Call us today for metallic epoxy flooring in Palo Alto.

Polyaspartic Floor Coating

Polyaspartic floor coating is a fast-curing, UV-stable alternative to standard epoxy. It can take foot traffic the same day and stays clear in direct sunlight instead of turning amber.

It suits homeowners who need the garage back quickly, garages that get afternoon sun through the door, and outdoor slabs like patios and entries. Standard epoxy in those spots tends to yellow in a band where the light hits.

Read more about polyaspartic floor coating. Call us today for polyaspartic floor coating in Palo Alto.

Commercial Epoxy Flooring

Commercial epoxy flooring covers labs, restaurant kitchens, offices, gyms and storage rooms with a coating matched to the chemicals, heat and traffic the floor sees. It gives bare concrete a sealed, washable surface that holds up to carts and spills.

Facility managers and business owners call before a move-in, during a tenant improvement, or when an old coating fails. Ignored, worn concrete keeps dusting, soaks up spills and gets harder to keep clean for inspections.

Read more about commercial epoxy flooring. Call us today for commercial epoxy flooring in Palo Alto.

Concrete Grinding and Crack Repair

Concrete grinding and crack repair is the prep work that every coating depends on. A diamond grinder strips old paint, sealer and stains, and cracks and chipped joints are filled and ground flush before any resin goes down.

Anyone with a cracked, pitted or previously painted floor needs this step, even if the plan is a simple clear sealer. Skip it, and cracks show through the new coating and old paint lets go, taking the new floor with it. Call us today for concrete grinding and crack repair in Palo Alto.
